What is CWC?

Coping with Caffeine is the guild's own addon. It keeps the roster up to date automatically, so you can see who is online, what they play, which professions they have and who is someone's alt, without asking around.

It is built for the guild only. It is not on a public addon store and it does not talk to any outside server.

What it does

Roster

Every character in the guild with race, class, level, item level, guild rank and role. Sort by name, race, level or status, and search by name, class, spec or profession.

Professions at a glance

Profession icons appear beside each name, so you can see who can craft or gather what. Pick a character to see their skill levels.

Main and alt links

Tag each character as a main or an alt. Alts link to their main, so you always know whose alt is whose.

Kill on Sight

Members can submit a target with a reason. Officers and the Guild Master approve or deny each request, and the decision reaches everyone who is online. If an approved target comes near you, CWC plays a warning sound and shows a Kill on Sight alert.

Player blacklist

The same request and approval process, for players the guild should avoid trading or grouping with.

Statistics

Counts of characters, mains and alts, plus the most common classes, races and professions in the guild.

Update notices

When a guild mate runs a newer version, CWC tells you in chat, with a popup and in its window, so nobody gets left on an old copy.

Raid PREVIEW

A work in progress. When raids become available, you'll be able to mark your interest in raiding with the guild and the roles you can fill.

How it works

When you log in, CWC reads your character and saves it on your computer. When you are online with other guild members who have the addon, you share your characters with each other, and the roster fills in. Nothing is sent to a website or a server.

  • Log in on your character. CWC scans it automatically. A setup screen asks whether it is a main or an alt on first use.
  • Stay online with guild mates. Their characters appear on your roster as soon as you are both online. Characters you have already received stay visible when those players are offline.
  • Submit or approve requests. Anyone can submit a request. Officers and Guild Masters approve or deny it, and everyone online sees the result.

Install

  1. Download the zip. Use the button at the top of this page.
  2. Extract the CWC folder into your WoW addons folder: World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\. The folder must be named CWC.
  3. Start the game and enable CWC on the addon list, then log in. Type /reload if it was already running.
  4. Find the minimap button and click it to open the roster. Right-click it for setup.

Updating: close the game, replace the CWC folder with the new one, and start again.

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone use CWC?

No. CWC is for members of the Coping with Caffeine guild only. It checks your guild when you log in, and if you're not a member it shows a message asking you to contact the Guild Master, Hello There.

Is CWC safe to install?

CWC only reads your own character details and shares them with guild members who have the addon. It does not ask for your password, your account details or any payment information, and it does not send anything to a website. The source code is public, so anyone can check it.

How will I know when there's an update?

Addons can't check websites, so CWC learns about new versions from guild mates. As soon as anyone online runs a newer version, CWC warns you in chat, shows a popup with the download link and adds a red banner to its window. The warning repeats at every login until you update. If you skip updates, guild sync can stop working and the roster may show missing or wrong data. This works from version 0.8.0 onwards, so anyone on an older version needs to update once by hand.

Why can't I see my guild mates' characters yet?

Characters are shared only while you and the other player are both online with the addon. Once you have received someone's characters, they stay on your roster, even when that person is offline. Ask a guild mate to log in while you are online.

Does it work with other addons?

Yes. When another addon has loaded LibDBIcon, the shared minimap button system most addons use, CWC's minimap button uses it too. Collector addons such as Minimap Button Button then show it alongside everything else.

How do I tag myself as a main or an alt?

Open the setup screen by right-clicking the minimap button, or from the Settings page. Choose main or alt, and for an alt, pick the name of its main.

Who can approve a Kill on Sight or blacklist request?

Only Guild Masters and Officers. Requests from anyone else are shown to everyone, but they stay pending until an officer approves or denies them.

My character shows the wrong class, race or professions. What do I do?

Log in on that character and use Rescan character on the Settings page. Then open Refresh guild data while your guild mates are online.

Where do I report a bug or suggest a change?

Tell an officer or post in the guild's Discord. Please include a screenshot and what you were doing when the problem happened.
