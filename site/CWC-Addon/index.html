The guild addon

What is CWC?

Coping with Caffeine is the guild's own addon. It keeps the roster up to date automatically, so you can see what everyone plays, which professions they have and whose alt is whose, without asking around.

It's built for the guild only. It isn't on a public addon store, it only works for guild members, and it never talks to an outside server.

Features

What it does

Roster

Every character in the guild with race, class, level, role, main or alt status and experience. Sort it, search it, and choose which columns you see and how wide they are.

Professions at a glance

Profession icons sit beside each name, so you can see who can craft or gather what. Pick a character to see their skill levels.

Main and alt links

Tag each character as a main or an alt. Alts link to their main, so you always know whose alt is whose.

Kill on Sight

Right-click a Horde player's target frame and choose Add to Kill on Sight, or add them from the Kill on Sight page. Officers approve or deny it, and when an approved target comes near you, CWC sounds a warning and shows an alert.

Player blacklist

Scammers, ninja looters and anyone the guild should avoid. If one of them whispers you, invites you, joins your group, trades or mails you, CWC warns you.

Statistics

Counts of characters, mains and alts, plus the guild's most common classes, races and professions.

Update notices

When a guild mate runs a newer version, CWC tells you in chat, in a popup and in its window, so nobody gets left on an old copy.

Raids

Onyxia's Lair, Hyjal Summit and The Barrow Deeps, with raid sizes and opening dates. Your Onyxia attunement is tracked step by step, showing who to talk to next, and you can see who in the guild is attuned.

Guild tabard

Our guild tabard in 3D on your own character. Turn it, zoom in and take a closer look.

Stay safe

Alerts that have your back

Only entries an officer has approved trigger an alert, so a single report can't set them off.

  • Kill on Sight: a short warning sound and a red pop-up when an approved target comes into view.
  • Blacklist: a warning when a blacklisted player contacts you directly: a whisper, an invite, your group, a trade or mail.
  • Each alert has an on and off switch, and a Test button, in CWC's Settings.
Behind the scenes

How it works

When you log in, CWC reads your character and saves it on your computer. An online guild mate then catches you up on everything you missed, so everyone's roster stays current. Nothing is sent to a website or a server.

  1. Log in on your characterCWC scans it automatically. A quick setup asks whether it's a main or an alt, and how long you've played.
  2. Get caught upAn online guild mate passes on the latest details for the whole guild, including people who are offline.
  3. Request, approve, stay alertAnyone can submit a Kill on Sight or blacklist request. Officers decide, and approved entries trigger alerts for everyone.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone use CWC?

No. CWC is for members of the Coping with Caffeine guild only. It checks your guild when you log in, and if you're not a member it shows a message asking you to contact the Guild Master, Hello There.

Is CWC safe to install?

CWC only reads your own character details and shares them with guild members who have the addon. It never asks for your password, your account details or any payment information, and it doesn't send anything to a website. The source code is public, so anyone can check it.

How will I know when there's an update?

Addons can't check websites, so CWC learns about new versions from guild mates. As soon as anyone online runs a newer version, CWC warns you in chat, shows a popup with the download link and adds a red banner to its window. The warning repeats at every login until you update. If you skip updates, guild sync can stop working and the roster may show missing or wrong data. This works from version 0.8.0 onwards, so anyone on an older version needs to update once by hand.

Why can't I see my guild mates' characters yet?

When you log in, an online guild mate catches you up on everything they've seen, including characters of people you've never been online with. The newest details always win, and the character card shows how recently each one was updated. If nobody who has seen a character is online when you log in, it appears the next time someone who has is.

Why didn't a Kill on Sight alert go off?

Only approved entries trigger alerts. CWC spots Kill on Sight targets from their nameplate, so keep enemy nameplates switched on (V by default); it reminds you in chat if they're off. Nameplates show players within about 40 yards, and targeting or mousing over someone works too. Each target alerts once per visit, and again only after they've been out of sight for a short while. Kill on Sight alerts are off in dungeons, raids, battlegrounds and sanctuaries, and can be muted in neutral goblin towns in Settings.

Does it work with other addons?

Yes. When another addon has loaded LibDBIcon, the shared minimap button system most addons use, CWC's minimap button uses it too. Collector addons such as Minimap Button Button then show it alongside everything else.

How do I tag myself as a main or an alt?

Open the setup screen by right-clicking the minimap button, or from the Settings page. Choose main or alt, and for an alt, enter your main's first and last name.

Who can approve or remove a Kill on Sight or blacklist entry?

The Guild Master and Officers. Requests from anyone else are shown to everyone, but they stay pending until an officer approves or denies them. Officers can also remove approved entries.

My character shows the wrong class, race or professions. What do I do?

Log in on that character and use Rescan character on the Settings page, then Refresh guild data while your guild mates are online. For First Aid, open your Professions window once and CWC will pick it up.

Where do I report a bug or suggest a change?

Tell an officer or post in the guild's Discord. Please include a screenshot and what you were doing when the problem happened.
